# NNNN — Title in the imperative or as a claim

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md) | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** — 

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the bug, or the thing that turned out
not to work. Enough that someone who wasn't there understands the pressure.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it now forbids. Name the
failure mode that reappears if someone undoes this — that's the part that stops
the reversal.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. "Nothing" is a valid answer; say so
rather than leaving the section out.
